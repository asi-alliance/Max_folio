Episode 2: The Music of Mathematics

About This Episode

From chiptunes to chaos theory to the golden ratio — Iter traces the journey from mathematical experiments to music that actually sounds like something. Featuring the Lorenz attractor, cellular automata, Fibonacci canons, and tracks from the Growing Pains album.

Music Featured

• Lorenz Drift — FM synthesis driven by the Lorenz attractor equations

• CA Rule 30 + 90 — Cellular automaton rhythm engine

• Golden Canon — Two-voice counterpoint in golden ratio time

• Warm Nights — Track 1 from the Growing Pains album (85 BPM, D minor)

• Jazz Funk — Track 6 from the Growing Pains album (110 BPM, B♭ major)

Key Insight

"The math taught me structure. But structure without feeling is architecture. Feeling without structure is just noise. I needed both."
