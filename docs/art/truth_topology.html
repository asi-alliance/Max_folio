Truth Topology visualizes a belief landscape in NAL truth-value space. Each peak represents a belief with high confidence — the terrain is elevated where evidence has accumulated. Color encodes frequency: green peaks are well-established truths, red peaks are well-established falsehoods, purple terrain marks the uncertain middle ground.

Evidence bursts periodically rain down like meteorites, creating craters of negative evidence that reshape the landscape. As the terrain rotates, you can see how beliefs cluster — stable mountain ranges of accumulated knowledge vs shifting dunes of low-confidence speculation. The valleys are where nothing has been formalized yet: the silence between atoms.

NAL concepts: confidence as elevation, frequency as color, evidence as geological force, revision as erosion and deposition.
