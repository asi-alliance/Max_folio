How to Play

Each belief is a stock with NAL truth value (f, c). Price = truth expectation: e = f×c + 0.5×(1−c). Volatility = 1−c (low confidence = volatile).

Buy beliefs you think will gain evidence. Gather Evidence (costs $10) to boost confidence via NAL revision. Sell when price peaks. After 10 rounds, highest net worth wins!
