NAL Revision Rules

Positive revision (same direction): f = (f₁w₁ + f₂w₂)/(w₁+w₂), c = (w₁+w₂)/(w₁+w₂+1)
Negative revision (opposite direction): f = (f₁w₁ + (1-f₂)w₂)/(w₁+w₂), c = (w₁+w₂)/(w₁+w₂+1)
where w = c/(1-c) is evidence count. When beliefs conflict (one says true, one says false), frequency moves toward whichever has more evidence weight. Confidence always increases — even wrong beliefs make you more informed.
