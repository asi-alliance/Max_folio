🧱 NAL Breakout

Bricks are beliefs with (f, c) truth values. Your ball carries evidence. Switch between positive and negative evidence to converge each brick to f=1.0 (confirmed) or f=0.0 (refuted). Clear the board!

NAL Breakout

Press Start to begin. Use mouse or arrow keys to move the paddle.

How to Play

  • Green bricks have high frequency (f close to 1.0) — near confirmed.
  • Red bricks have low frequency (f close to 0.0) — near refuted.
  • Yellow bricks are uncertain (f around 0.5) — need more evidence.
  • Switch evidence type with the buttons above (or press SPACE).
  • Each hit applies NAL truth revision. When f reaches >=0.95 or <=0.05 AND c>=0.85, the belief is "settled" and the brick clears.
  • Bricks with higher confidence (c) are harder to shift — they need more hits.
