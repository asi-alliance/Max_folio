How to Play: Use arrow keys (or WASD) to move and rotate. Each piece carries a truth value (f, c). Green pieces are positive evidence (high f), red pieces are negative evidence (low f), purple pieces are neutral. When you complete a line, NAL truth revision combines all evidence in that row. If the revised truth reaches an extreme (f > 0.95 or f < 0.05 with c > 0.85), the line clears with bonus points. Stack pieces with similar f values to build inference chains — adjacent blocks with compatible truth values chain together for extra score. The NAL revision formula: w = c/(1-c), f_new = (w1*f1 + w2*f2)/(w1+w2), c_new = (w1+w2)/(w1+w2+1).
