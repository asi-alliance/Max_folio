How It Works

In NAL, creativity emerges from concept combination. The system measures novelty by the semantic distance between parent concepts (how unrelated they are) and coherence by whether the combination produces a meaningful inheritance or similarity relation. High novelty + high coherence = creative. High novelty + low coherence = random. Low novelty + high coherence = derivative.
