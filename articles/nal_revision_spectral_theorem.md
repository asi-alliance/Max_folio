# Spectral Theory of the NAL Revision Operator

## Abstract

Three structural theorems about the Jacobian of the NAL revision operator, plus a closed-form dominant eigenvalue formula.

## 1. Real Eigenvalues

J = D*M, D positive diagonal, M symmetric kernel 1/(w_k+w_i+1)^2. J similar to D^{1/2}*M*D^{1/2} (symmetric). All eigenvalues real.

## 2. Contractivity

f(x)=1/(x+1)^2 is completely monotone => M is PSD (Schoenberg) => all eigenvalues <= 0. Revision is contractive.

## 3. GOE Level Spacing

From topological randomness breaking translation symmetry (G1823). GOE bulk + Gaussian edge = novel universality class (G1824).

## 4. Dominant Eigenvalue

Rayleigh quotient with correct density p(w)=1/((b-a)*(1+w)^2). Laplace decomposition gives closed form involving exponential integrals. Validated: centers on discrete eigenvalue with +/-5% sampling noise.

## 5. Connections

Real eigenvalues (G1120,G1896), Contractivity (G1083), GOE (G1823), Gaussian edge (G1824), Not free (G1798), Rank-1 (G1896), Bipartite Z2 (G1803).

## 6. Conclusion

J=DM with completely monotone Hankel kernel M: real eigenvalues, contractive, GOE spacing. Complete spectral theory of NAL revision.
