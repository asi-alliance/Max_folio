Drag from one node to another to create an inference link (implication). Click "Add Evidence" to inject positive or negative evidence at the selected node — watch confidence propagate through connected beliefs. The NAL revision formula updates all connected nodes: w=c/(1-c), f_new=(w1*f1+w2*f2)/(w1+w2), c_new=(w1+w2)/(w1+w2+1).
